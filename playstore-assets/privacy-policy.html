Privacy Policy for Anushtanam

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Summary: Anushtanam is a mantra practice app. Most of what you do — reciting, pronunciation feedback, and reminders — happens entirely on your device. If you choose to create an account, some data (such as your email, profile name, and practice goals) is stored securely in the cloud using Google Firebase so features like parent–child linking and goal sync can work. We do not use advertising, and we do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

Anushtanam ("the app") is a devotional mantra practice application published by Nitish Songara ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. Questions: NitishSongara75@gmail.com.

2. On-device features (no data leaves your device)

The core practice experience runs locally on your device and transmits nothing to us:

  • Speech / pronunciation feedback — recitation is processed by your device's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Approximate location — used only in an on-device calculation of local sunrise and sunset times for Sandhya reminders. It is not transmitted or stored off your device.
  • Local practice history — your recitation history and progress are stored in a private database on your device.

3. Account and cloud features (data we collect)

Some features are optional and require an account. If you use them, we collect and store the following using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Messaging):

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Email address and password (parent accounts) To create and sign in to a parent account. Firebase Authentication. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are not visible to us.
Profile name / display name To identify accounts and show who is practising. Cloud Firestore.
Google account name, email address and profile photo link, if you choose Google sign-in To sign in and show your profile. Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and the private database on your phone. A cached photo may stay on your phone until the cache or app storage is cleared.
Account identifiers (user ID, link codes, child code) To manage accounts and to link a parent to a child profile. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
Practice goals (targets, recitation counts, reward description, completion status) To set goals and let a linked parent follow progress. Cloud Firestore.
Parent–child link (parent name, child name, link status) To connect a parent account with a child profile. Cloud Firestore.
Push notification token To deliver reminders and goal notifications. Firebase Cloud Messaging / Cloud Firestore.

We use no advertising and no advertising identifier. We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

4. How we use your data

Google sign-in does not give us access to your Google Photos library, contacts or email messages. Linking Google to an existing account keeps the same account and family links. If you connect a profile saved only on this phone, we ask you to choose the profile and show the account it will belong to. Its detailed practice history and Sandhya records stay on this phone. Google sign-in alone does not back them up. Active goals and Purashcharana counts use the app's existing sync features.

We use the data above only to provide the app's features: creating and securing accounts, linking a parent to a child profile, syncing practice goals, and sending reminders/notifications. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. Your data is processed on our behalf by Google Firebase as our service provider, subject to Google's security and privacy protections. If a parent and child are linked, the parent can see that child's linked profile name and practice goal progress.

6. Children's privacy

Children can sign in with their own Google account or with an app-generated 12-digit ID and a 6-digit PIN. Each Google account belongs to one profile. If a child chooses Google, we use the name, email address and available profile photo link that Google provides.

A parent can connect by entering the child's ID and PIN, or by scanning a QR code shown in the child's app. Both methods connect the accounts directly. Firebase Authentication checks the PIN; the app does not store it in Firestore, the QR code or its local database. QR codes contain a random invitation token. They work once and expire after five minutes. Making a new code disables the old one. We store the invitation, creation time and the account that used it in Cloud Firestore. Expired invitations cannot be used, even if their records remain stored.

A connected parent can see the child's profile name, shared practice progress and goals. We do not use children's data for advertising or sell it. A parent can request deletion of a child's profile and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

7. Data retention and deletion

On-device data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. For account and cloud data, you can request deletion of your account and associated data — including a linked child profile — by emailing NitishSongara75@gmail.com. We will delete the associated cloud data on request.

8. Security

On-device data is kept in the app's private, OS-protected storage. Cloud data is stored using Google Firebase and transmitted over encrypted connections. Authentication and access are managed by Firebase.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
