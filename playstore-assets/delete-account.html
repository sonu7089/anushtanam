Delete Your Anushtanam Account and Data

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Anushtanam account and the data associated with it. Anushtanam is published by Nitish Songara.

How to request deletion

To delete your account and its associated data, email NitishSongara75@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Anushtanam account".

Please send the request from, or include, the email address used for the account. If you are a parent requesting deletion of a linked child profile, mention the child profile name or link code.

What gets deleted

On receiving your request, we delete the following data stored in the cloud (Google Firebase):

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (Firebase Authentication).
  • Your profile name, account identifiers and any saved Google profile photo link.
  • Your practice goals, japa and Sandhya history, recitation performance, corrections, conflict history and restore records stored in the cloud.
  • Any parent–child link, and — if you are the parent — the linked child profile's data.
  • Your notification (push) token.
  • Your family QR invitations, including expired invitations and records of their use.

Data stored only on your device (your local practice history) is removed when you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Deleting your Anushtanam account does not delete your Google account. Any profile photo cached on your phone is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Timeframe

We process deletion requests and remove the associated cloud data within 30 days. We do not retain the deleted data after that, except where retention is required by law.

Contact
