Delete Your Anushtanam Account and Data

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Anushtanam account and the data associated with it. Anushtanam is published by Nitish Songara.

How to request deletion

In the app, open Settings > Your account and privacy > Delete my account. Read what will be removed. We will then ask you to sign in with Google again or enter your account password to make sure it's you. Once we receive your request, it cannot be canceled. Access to your online account stops while we remove it and its saved information. You can sign out, then clear the app's storage in Android Settings to remove practice saved on your phone.

You can also request deletion of your account and its associated data by emailing NitishSongara75@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Anushtanam account".

Please send the request from, or include, the email address used for the account. If you are a parent requesting deletion of a child's separate account, mention the child profile name. We will need to check the account and that you are allowed to make the request. Please do not send passwords or QR codes.

What gets deleted

When we receive your request, we remove this information saved online with Google Firebase:

  • Your Anushtanam account and its sign-in details.
  • Your profile name, account identifiers and any saved Google profile photo link.
  • Your practice goals, japa and Sandhya history, recitation results and corrections. This includes copies kept to resolve sync problems or restore your practice.
  • Your synced settings, selected reminder location, profile display choices, and unfinished count entries.
  • Family links and shared goals involving your account. Other linked accounts and their private history remain.
  • The device details used to send you notifications.
  • Your family QR invitations, including expired invitations and records of their use.
  • Older child sign-in codes and any notification test registrations for your device.

Data stored only on your device (your local practice history) is removed when you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Deleting your Anushtanam account does not delete your Google account. Any profile photo cached on your phone is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Timeframe

We remove your account and its saved online information within 30 days. We keep a short record of the request for two hours after deletion to stop older sign-ins from restoring access. That record is removed during the next hourly check. If deletion is interrupted, our system tries again.

Contact
