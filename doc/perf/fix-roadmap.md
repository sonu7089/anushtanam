# Fix roadmap

This is the master implementation order for the findings in `doc/perf/`. It supersedes the separate implementation sequences in the individual audits. Those files retain the evidence, source links and detailed acceptance checks; this document tracks the work without repeating them.

**Status: steps 00–09 implemented and locally verified; steps 10–13 remain open. Step 02's rules were deployed to `anushtanam-shastralife` on 12 September 2026 at the user's request.** Check an item only after its changes and relevant verification pass. Repeated findings across audits are grouped into one work item. Rendering/index candidates require measurement: close them with either a verified improvement or a documented reason to retain the current behavior.

## Dependencies and completion rules

Use the order above by default. Steps 01–04 are separate correctness fixes; they can be prepared independently after baseline setup. The isolated splash, image and cache-first Messages changes can land earlier once their own baseline/verification is available. This does not relax the dependencies for counting, schema or sync changes.

Do not remove repair/capture safeguards until the replacement durable events and recovery path are proven. Do not add uniqueness constraints before repairing incompatible rows. Do not remove required speech timers, notification de-duplication or account gates to improve a performance metric. Existing protections and false positives are listed in the [redundant-work audit](redundant-work-audit.md).

For each completed item, append its change/PR link and verification evidence to that checklist entry. Keep measured results in the detailed audit rather than expanding this roadmap. A rules-probe pass currently reproduces gaps; it is not proof that the rules are safe. Emulator-only and synthetic SQLite results also do not replace Android/device validation.
