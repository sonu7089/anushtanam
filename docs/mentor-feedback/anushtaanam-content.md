# Anushtaanam and the app's voice

Content direction shared by the app owner on 24 September 2026.

Use **Anushtaanam** for the inward practice described by the app. The Sandhya
heading is "A few moments for Anushtaanam." Use "Sandhya sessions" when counting
completed sessions, so the meaning of a number stays clear.

The owner's explanation distinguishes prayer arising from fear, worship through
remembering Paramatma's qualities, and Anushtaanam as a simple personal practice
for realising the oneness of Jivatma and Paramatma. This Advaita understanding
guides the app's content. It is not a reason to judge how someone else practises.

The parent playing hide and seek with a child is the owner's example. Paramatma
hides and Jivatma searches. The search ends in an embrace and the recognition of
oneness. The body, life and universe are described as maya, the appearance of
separation. The practice guide introduces this in short, everyday sentences.

Write as someone helping a person they care about:

- Use simple English and explain Sanskrit terms when introducing them.
- Keep everyday prompts short. Put longer reflections in the practice guide.
- Encourage people to return at their own pace. Avoid guilt and pressure over counts.
- Offer concrete help instead of grand promises or generic praise.
- Use full stops and commas. Do not use em dashes in prose.
- Keep the warmth natural. Read the words aloud before adding them to the app.

This wording change concerns the practice terminology. Existing app identifiers,
Firebase project names and published brand references remain Anushtanam.
